Visual identity brainstorm inputs for The Marrow of Empire (delivered as a Visual Identity Statement draft on 2026-08-08):

- Anchor: dark fantasy Gothic — fallen empire in Gothic stone, jagged spires, crumbling archways, haunted forests. Mood line: "Every scene should feel like a dark fairy tale — beautiful and dangerous in equal measure, where the ruins whisper and the forest watches."
- Touchstones: Dark Souls (ruined grandeur, environmental storytelling through decay), Diablo 1-2 (gothic dark fantasy, isometric, moody lighting, corrupted sacred spaces), LOTR films (nature reclaiming stone, ancient scale), One Piece Void Century (lost advanced civilization whose tech now reads as myth/magic)
- Key synthesis requirement: the empire's lost technology must read as mythic/magical, NEVER sci-fi. Load-bearing idea: "legend filter" — design artifacts the way a fairy tale would describe them (function can be technological, appearance must be mythic). The empire fell so completely its peak is legend.
- Supporting principles from brainstorm: dark fantasy not horror (beauty persists in decay); nature as reclamation (roots crack stone, moss veils statues); atmosphere over fidelity; readable tactical space
- Warmth directive (user decision 2026-08-08): warm gold leads the world's default register — "adventure soul". The Drakensang: The River of Time is the warmth anchor (painterly, golden, lived-in world — already in game-concept inspiration table). Cold and pale are events, not the baseline; the revelation collision's power comes from scarcity. Day = golden baseline; weather cools a warm world. User confirmed this during Section 3 authoring.
- Color: deep earth tones punctuated by dramatic contrast — golden lantern light, pale dusk glow, cold mountain shadow
- Asset foundation: KitBash3D Dark Fantasy pack (Gothic architecture, ruined structures)

Section 9 (Style Prohibitions) key decisions: 8 prohibitions, each naming the thing never done, the rule it would break (cited), and the true version instead; gate rule — new visual ideas judged by "which rule does it keep?", rule-breaking additions require amending the art bible in writing. (1) Never sci-fi — legend filter (1.1) bans plasma/neon/holograms/digital displays/chrome; the ban is on first-sight reading — the machine may live under the surface (Principle 3, 2.6), so empire tech reads as holy relics/oracle stones/impossible geometry; (2) never generic fantasy — no dragons/elves/glowing magic swords/fireballs/wizard robes; supernatural vocabulary closed to 4.1's four hostile families; investigator = not-a-chosen-one with journal + modest blade (4.5); spells act "the way the world breathes" (7.1); (3) never horror/gore for its own sake — no jump scares/gratuitous gore/body-horror decoration; horror notes rationed by design (4.1/7.2/4.6); blood-on-ivory is always a clue (2.2); (4) never UI outside the flat register — no 3D UI, no diegetic HUD floating in world space, no skeuomorphic buttons (6.4/6.3); flat world-anchored HUD remains (6.1) — the ban targets UI that pretends to live inside the painting; (5) never procedural generation — no procedural dungeons/random NPCs/generated quests; composition authored (5.6), witness budget (5.3), moss authored per-asset (5.2); "a seed is not a writer"; (6) never filler combat — no random encounters/respawns/XP grind; respawn contradicts Pillar 2 world-keeps-the-mark (5.4) and the finite-violence effects contract (7.2/7.3); (7) never comedy/parody/meta — no self-referential jokes/fourth-wall breaks/ironic detachment; warmth = human moments (State 2, 4.3 idles), not jokes; (8) never pure black or pure white — #000000/#FFFFFF banned everywhere (world, UI, text); soot #141210 warm (2.1), bone ivory #E9E6DC (2.6), ink #1C1813 (2.4), ghost glow #F5F3EC the ceiling; pure black = void not unknown, pure white = sterile not sacred.
